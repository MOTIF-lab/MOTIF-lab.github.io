PhD student Yuhang Wang received a Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL) 2026 Travel Award for **Observe Before You Alert: Adaptive Driver Alerting with Vision–Language Models**, written with Lingyao Li (University of Arizona) and Hao Zhou. The paper casts driver alerting as a per-tick policy over SILENT, OBSERVE and ALERT and releases VLAlert-Bench; see the [project page](/projects/vlalert/).
