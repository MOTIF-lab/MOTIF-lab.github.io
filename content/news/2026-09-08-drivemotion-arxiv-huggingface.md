DriveMotion standardises driver body motion from BATON, web dashcam uploads and AIDE into one skeleton-only benchmark: 9,010 sequences, 680,082 forecasting windows, 360 drivers. Arm motion in the seconds before a manoeuvre is 3.4× its baseline amplitude, and forecasting from the skeleton improves part-state F1 by 44 % over a zero-motion reference. The paper and the gated CC BY 4.0 release are linked from the [project page](/projects/drivemotion/).
