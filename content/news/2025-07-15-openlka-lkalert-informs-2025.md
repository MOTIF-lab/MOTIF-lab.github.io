The abstract **From OpenLKA to LKAlert** was accepted to the INFORMS Annual Meeting 2025 in Atlanta, in the Intelligent Transportation Systems and Cybersecurity session. It connects the [OpenLKA](/projects/openlka/) dataset of production lane-keeping behavior to predictive alerting for lane-keeping-assist systems.
