**DriveDNA** (Yuhang Wang, Lingyao Li, Hao Zhou) is on arXiv. The benchmark shows how much of a high re-identification score is really a route shortcut — a video-only probe falls from .937 to .675 once contexts are matched — and ships frozen folds, a research license with manual approval and an auto-approved sample. Explore the driver map and the leakage ladder on the [project page](/projects/drivedna/).
