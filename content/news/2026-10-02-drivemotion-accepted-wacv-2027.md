**DriveMotion: A Large-Scale Multi-Source Benchmark for Driver Motion Sequence Modeling and Forecasting** (Yuhang Wang, Chuheng Wei, Jingxin Yang, Xishun Liao, Hao Zhou) has been accepted to the IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV 2027), to be held in Lake Buena Vista, Florida, in January 2027.

The release standardises 400 hours of in-cabin driver motion from 360 drivers as 133-keypoint skeleton sequences at 10 Hz — no face or cabin pixels — across three sources, and benchmarks forecasting seconds ahead of real manoeuvres. The interactive [project page](/projects/drivemotion/) has the skeleton player, the leaderboard and the access terms (CC BY 4.0, gated).
