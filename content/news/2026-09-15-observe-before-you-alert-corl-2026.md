**Observe Before You Alert: Adaptive Driver Alerting with Vision–Language Models** (Yuhang Wang, Lingyao Li, Hao Zhou) has been accepted to the Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL 2026). The OBSERVE action lets the system gather evidence before it warns; on VLAlert-Bench validation the policy reaches a DAUS of 0.4878 versus 0.4752 for Open-BADAS, and on 221 held-out ADAS-TO-Critic clips it raises R@5s from 74.2 % to 88.7 %. Demos, tables and the benchmark's access terms are on the [project page](/projects/vlalert/).
