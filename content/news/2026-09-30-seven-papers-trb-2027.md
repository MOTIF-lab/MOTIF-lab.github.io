Seven papers from the lab were accepted for presentation at the Transportation Research Board (TRB) 106th Annual Meeting in Washington, D.C., January 2027. Five are first-authored by Yuhang Wang — the cockpit world model behind [TriDrive](/projects/tridrive/) (CockpitWM), [Observe Before You Alert](/projects/vlalert/), [BATON](/projects/baton/), *Human Takeovers in Level-2 ADAS* ([ADAS-TO](/projects/adas-to/)) and [DriveDNA](/projects/drivedna/) — and two are co-authored with lab members and collaborators.
