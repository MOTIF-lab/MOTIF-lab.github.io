Every release below was built from production vehicles driven by real people on public roads: lane keeping assist and takeovers recorded on the drivers' own cars, driver motion rendered as skeletons, and dashcam alert decisions scored tick by tick. Each card opens the full interactive project page; the table compares the releases side by side, and the access tiers say exactly how to get the data.
