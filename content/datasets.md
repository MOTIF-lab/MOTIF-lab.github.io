MOTIF Lab releases the data behind its papers so others can build on it. Each dataset below links to its download page, paper, and code. Some downloads on Hugging Face ask you to sign in and accept the dataset terms first.

## OpenLKA — lane keeping assist on production cars

The first open dataset of lane-keeping-assist behavior from a wide range of production vehicles in real driving, pairing CAN-bus signals with synchronized front-camera video and lane-perception output.

[Project page](https://wangyuhang-cmd.github.io/openlka/) · [Paper (arXiv:2501.03287)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03287) · [Code](https://github.com/OpenLKA/OpenLKA) · [More about the project](/projects/openlka/)

## ADAS-TO — driver takeovers during ADAS

Over **15,000 real-world takeover events** from **327 drivers** across **22 vehicle brands**, with synchronized video and vehicle telemetry for each event.

[Download (Hugging Face)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HenryYHW/ADAS-TO) · [Paper (arXiv:2603.06986)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06986) · [Project page](https://wangyuhang-cmd.github.io/adasto/) · [Code](https://github.com/OpenLKA/ADAS-TO) · [More about the project](/projects/adas-to/)

## BATON — two-way driver–automation handoffs

A multimodal benchmark covering both directions of control transition — handing control to automation and taking it back — from **136.6 hours** of naturalistic driving by **127 drivers** across **380 routes**.

[Download (Hugging Face)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HenryYHW/BATON) · [Paper (arXiv:2604.07263)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07263) · [Project page](https://wangyuhang-cmd.github.io/baton/) · [Code](https://github.com/OpenLKA/BATON) · [More about the project](/projects/baton/)

## DriveDNA — driving-style identification

Over **4,100 drives** from **465 drivers** across **115 vehicle models** (nearly 1,000 hours), built to separate a driver's own style from vehicle and environment effects.

[Download (Hugging Face)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HenryYHW/DriveDNA) · [Paper (arXiv:2607.23822)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23822) · [Project page](https://wangyuhang-cmd.github.io/drivedna/) · [Code](https://github.com/WangYuHang-cmd/DriveDNA) · [More about the project](/projects/drivedna/)

## DriveMotion — in-cabin driver motion forecasting

**400 hours** of in-cabin skeleton motion from **360 drivers** (9,010 sequences, 133 keypoints per frame at 10 Hz), standardized across sources, with a forecasting task: watch 8 seconds, predict the next 4. Released under CC BY 4.0.

[Download (Hugging Face)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HenryYHW/DriveMotion) · [Paper (arXiv:2609.08117)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08117) · [Project page](https://wangyuhang-cmd.github.io/drivemotion/) · [More about the project](/projects/drivemotion/)

## VLAlert-Bench — per-second driver alert decisions

**13,534 videos** and **192,892 labeled one-second clips** pooled from six driving-event datasets. For each second, a model decides whether to stay silent, keep watching, or alert the driver. Released under CC BY 4.0.

[Download (Hugging Face)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HenryYHW/VLAlert) · [Project page](https://wangyuhang-cmd.github.io/vlalert/) · [More about the project](/projects/vlalert/)
